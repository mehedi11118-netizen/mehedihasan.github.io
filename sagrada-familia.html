Sagrada Familia

Architecture Translated into Fashion

Inspired by the extraordinary architectural language of Sagrada Familia, this project explores the relationship between architecture, form, structure and fashion design. Through visual research and creative exploration, architectural details are interpreted as a source of inspiration for contemporary fashion expression.
