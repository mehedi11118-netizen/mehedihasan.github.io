01 / Project Overview

Architecture translated into fashion.

This project explores the relationship between architecture and contemporary fashion. The visual identity of Sagrada Familia serves as a creative reference for exploring form, structure, silhouette and decorative details. Through fashion illustration and digital garment development, architectural inspiration is translated into a wearable design direction.

02 / Research & Inspiration

Finding inspiration in structure.

The research process focuses on architectural forms, geometric compositions, visual rhythm and decorative elements. These references help establish the creative direction and design language of the project.

03 / Design Development

From concept to digital design.

The design development process combines visual research, fashion illustration and digital garment visualization. Adobe Illustrator and CLO 3D support the translation of initial ideas into developed fashion concepts.
