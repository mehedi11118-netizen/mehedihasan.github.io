01 / The Concept

Where architecture meets fashion.

Inspired by the distinctive architectural language of Sagrada Familia, this project explores the translation of architectural structures and artistic details into a fashion design direction. The creative process investigates form, proportion, visual rhythm and material expression.

02 / Research & Inspiration

Observing forms.
Discovering details.

Architectural references, geometric compositions and decorative elements establish the visual direction of the project. The research process provides a foundation for exploring new silhouettes and surface expressions.

03 / Design Development

From visual research
to design expression.

The development process brings together fashion illustration and digital visualization. Initial ideas are explored through creative development and translated into a considered fashion design concept.
